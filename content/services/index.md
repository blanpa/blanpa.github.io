## How I Approach It

<div class="process-steps">
  <div class="process-step glass">
    <span class="process-step__num">01</span>
    <h4>Understand the plant</h4>
    <p>What is actually installed, what it speaks, and which constraints are non-negotiable.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="process-step glass">
    <span class="process-step__num">02</span>
    <h4>Prove the connection</h4>
    <p>Get one real value out of the machine before designing anything around it.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="process-step glass">
    <span class="process-step__num">03</span>
    <h4>Build in the open</h4>
    <p>Iterate in small steps, with the flows and the reasoning visible along the way.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="process-step glass">
    <span class="process-step__num">04</span>
    <h4>Hand it over</h4>
    <p>Documentation and a system somebody else can maintain without me.</p>
  </div>
</div>
